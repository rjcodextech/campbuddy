# What is CampBuddy?

CampBuddy is a digital companion for people attending a WordCamp — the free, community-run conferences that happen all over the world for people who use and build with WordPress.

Think of it as the friend who's been to a dozen WordCamps already, walking in next to you on your first one. It doesn't just show you a schedule — it tells you what to actually *do* while you're there.

---

## The problem it solves

Walking into your first WordCamp can be overwhelming. You don't know anyone, you're not sure what "Contributor Day" even means, you don't know who to talk to or how to start a conversation, and most event apps just dump a schedule on you and call it a day.

CampBuddy exists so nobody has to figure that out alone.

---

## What CampBuddy does

- **Guides you through the day** — tells you what's happening right now and what you should be doing about it, instead of leaving you to piece it together yourself.
- **Gives you a simple checklist** — small, doable things like "say hi to someone from another city" or "visit the sponsor area" that turn an intimidating room full of strangers into a game you can actually win.
- **Helps you find your people** — you tell it what you're into and who you'd love to meet, and it introduces you to other attendees at the same event who are looking for the same kind of connection.
- **Builds you a digital identity card** — a shareable card with your name, what you do, and a QR code that connects straight to your LinkedIn, so meeting someone new doesn't end with a scrap of paper you'll lose. Pick from a handful of card designs to match your style.
- **Plans your day with you** — browse the sessions, save the ones you care about, and get a nudge a few minutes before they start. On a multi-day WordCamp, each day gets its own list, so you're never scrolling through a jumbled schedule.
- **Points you toward ways to contribute** — WordCamps aren't just talks; there's a whole volunteer side (writing, testing, design, support, and more). CampBuddy asks a few simple questions and points you to where you'd actually enjoy helping out.
- **Surfaces real deals** — sponsors sometimes offer attendees a discount on their tools or services, and CampBuddy shows you those offers in one place instead of you having to go hunting for them. A few deals ask for a quick name and email first, so the sponsor knows who to follow up with — that's each sponsor's own choice, not the norm.

---

## How it works, from your side

1. **You open the app** — no account, no signup, no password. It just works, right in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen like any other app.
2. **You tell it a little about yourself, if you want** — what you're into, who you'd like to meet. Every question is skippable, and it stays private to you unless you choose to use the matching or sharing features.
3. **You pick your event** — CampBuddy shows you a short list of upcoming WordCamps to choose from. Once you pick one, everything you see (schedule, people, sponsors) is specific to that event.
4. **It guides you** — a home screen that always tells you what's relevant right now, a checklist to work through, a schedule to plan, and people to meet.
5. **You use it during the event** — check off your progress, bookmark sessions, get reminders, show your Camp Card when you meet someone new.
6. **You keep what you want, and nothing you don't** — everything you entered stays on your phone. You can export it or wipe it clean whenever you like.

---

## Who it's for

- **First-time attendees**, who need the most guidance and reassurance that they're "doing it right."
- **Returning attendees**, who still want an easy way to plan their day and meet new people.
- **Event organizers**, who can hand this to their attendees as a ready-made companion for their specific WordCamp — no need to build anything themselves. CampBuddy can even find and set up a new WordCamp on its own once the event's site is live and its first attendees have registered, ready for an organizer to review.

---

## Why it's different

- **No account, ever.** You don't sign up, you don't log in, and nobody's collecting your personal information to use it.
- **It's honest about privacy.** Anything personal you type in stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.
- **It works even with bad wifi.** Once you've opened it, the core of the app keeps working offline — which matters a lot at a crowded venue.
- **It feels like it belongs to the event.** Each WordCamp's own name and logo greet you inside the app, so it feels like an official part of that event, not a generic third-party tool.
- **It keeps you in the app.** Tap a sponsor or a deal and it opens right there, in place, instead of bouncing you out to a separate browser tab and losing your spot.
- **It's free**, made by the WordPress community, for the WordPress community.
