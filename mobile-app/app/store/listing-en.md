## Full description
CampBuddy is the free companion for WordCamp.

No sign-up. No account. Open it, pick your WordCamp and you are in.

PLAN YOUR DAY
- See what is on now and next, with rooms and times
- Star the talks you want; get a reminder ten minutes before each one, even with no signal
- A warning when two saved sessions overlap

MEET YOUR PEOPLE
- Find attendees who share your interests and wave to swap names
- Share your Camp Card with a QR code, so there are no paper cards to lose
- Report or block anyone from the People list

MAKE THE MOST OF IT
- Quests and a checklist for first-timers
- Contributor Day tables, sponsor deals and free steals
- After the event: photos, talk recordings and a PDF of your day

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Pages you opened stay available offline, and your saved sessions are kept on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your schedule, notes and people you met stay on your phone. Nothing is sold, and there is no tracking across other apps.

CampBuddy is made for the WordPress community.
