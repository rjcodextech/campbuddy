## Main post

Pehli WordCamp mein sabse mushkil kya hota hai?

Talks nahi. Sabse mushkil hai ek aise hall mein khade hona jahan aap kisi ko nahi jaante. 😅

Kai first-timers yahi bolte hain:
→ schedule samajh nahi aata
→ baat kaise shuru karein, pata nahi
→ aur "Contributor Day" kya hota hai, ye bhi clear nahi

Isliye humne CampBuddy banaya. WordCamp attendees ke liye ek free companion app.

60 second ke is video mein poora app dekho 👇

🏠 Home: abhi kya chal raha hai, aur us waqt kya karna theek rahega
🗓️ My Day: sessions save karo, shuru hone se pehle reminder
✅ Quest: chhote kaam jaise "doosre shehar wale se milo", jo baat shuru karwa dete hain
🤝 Matching: same interests wale attendees se milo
🪪 Camp Card: naam, kaam aur QR code, scan karte hi seedha LinkedIn
🙋 Contribute: Contributor Day pe kaunsi team aapke liye sahi hai
🎁 Sponsors aur deals, ek hi jagah

Na account, na sign-up. Phone ke browser mein khulta hai, aur venue ka wifi kamzor ho tab bhi kaam karta hai.
