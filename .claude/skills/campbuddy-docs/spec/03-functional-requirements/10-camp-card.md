# 3.10 Camp Card

[← Index](00-index.md) · Previous: [3.9 Contribute](09-contribute.md) · Next: [3.11 Onboarding →](11-onboarding.md)

The attendee's portable digital identity — should read as a modern conference badge, not a form.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| CC1 | Fields: profile image, name, role/title, company/community, city, WordPress interests, "ask me about," LinkedIn, personal website, WordPress.org profile, and a small set of other explicitly supported social links. All optional except name. |
| CC2 | The attendee explicitly chooses which filled-in fields actually appear on the card — filling a field in doesn't automatically put it on the visible card. Onboarding answers ([3.11](11-onboarding.md)) are never surfaced here automatically ([8.3](../08-security-privacy.md#83-matching-data--the-local-by-default-shared-only-by-choice-architecture)'s privacy boundary applies to Camp Card too, not only to matching). |
| CC3 | A QR code is generated from the field the attendee designates as primary — LinkedIn is the obvious default suggestion, but the attendee can point it at any of their chosen links. |
| CC4 | The QR/card must be easy to hand to someone else to scan, without extra steps. |
| CC5 | Camp Card data (drafts and published state) is local-only ([1.0](../01-project-overview.md#10-product-philosophy)), same as onboarding — nothing about it reaches a server except what the attendee separately chooses to expose via Explore → People matching ([3.4](04-matching.md)), which is a distinct, separately-opted-into action. |
| CC6 | A real empty state for a Camp Card that's not filled in yet ([4.3](../04-non-functional-requirements.md#43-error-handling--empty-states)): *"Your Camp Card is almost ready — add your name and a link you'd like people to scan."* |

> **Current implementation notes:**
> - CC6's empty state was replaced with an always-visible **sample preview** (placeholder name/role/tags) shown until the attendee has real data, so they can see how the card looks before filling anything in.
> - All **6 layouts** (Classic, Minimal, Bold, Split, Badge, Pass) render at once as a **gallery** (`#camp-card-scroll` — a swipeable row on phones, a wrapping grid of equal 280–340px columns from 640px up), each with its own Share/Download buttons — there is no single-layout picker or "active" layout to select or persist; same underlying data (CC1), 6 simultaneous visual treatments.
> - **Pass** (the 6th layout) is modeled on a physical lanyard pass: diagonal two-tone ribbon corners, a lanyard-hole cutout, the event's **full logo** (`Event::logoUrl()`, falling back to `/media/logo.png`) instead of a small icon, CampBuddy's **full wordmark** (`/media/logo.svg`) as a footer watermark instead of its square icon, and a static **"Code is Poetry"** signature band (WordPress's own tagline, italic serif) along the bottom edge.
> - **Role and company only ever appear once.** They have their own dedicated line above the tags (shown only if the attendee actually chose "Role"/"Company" in "Show on my visible card" — CC2's opt-in applies to them too, not just the other fields) and are explicitly excluded from the tag-pill list below, which previously could double-show "Role" as both the dedicated line's text and a separate pill.
> - The other 5 layouts show the **event's own icon** (`Event::faviconUrl()`, falling back to the app's shared default favicon) near the top in place of a generic "Camp Card" label, and **CampBuddy's own logo** as a small watermark in the footer under the QR (see the wordmark bullet below — it used to be the square icon). Logos sit in a small white chip (`.camp-card__event-mark` / `.camp-card__brand-mark`) so they stay legible against any layout's background without per-layout color overrides.
> - **Every card is exactly the same size**: a fixed **3:5** shape (`aspect-ratio`), identical in all 6 layouts and at every screen size. Everything inside is sized in `cqw` (1% of the card's own width, via container query units — `_camp-card.scss`'s `cc()` helper converts from the original 300px design) so type, spacing and artwork scale with the card: fully responsive, and a 240px phone card, a 340px desktop card and the print export are the same layout. Because the height is fixed, a long tag list can't grow the card — `paintTags()` in `camp-card.js` shows as many pills as fit and folds the rest into a "+N" pill. With no QR (the sample preview) the content is vertically centered instead.
> - The QR embeds the **event's icon in its center** at error-correction level `H` (~30% recoverable, safe headroom for the small covered area). It's drawn at whole pixels per module (so module edges stay crisp) — ~320px for the on-screen card, ~720px for the export. The QR frame and footer layout (centered column: QR, then the brand mark below it) are **identical across all 6 layouts**.
> - Every card has a visible **charcoal border** (`.camp-card` base rule, 3px at the 300px design size) so it reads as a distinct object against the page background regardless of the card's own fill color.
> - **Share** (Web Share API, with a file-sharing check and download fallback for browsers without it) and **Download** are the only two actions — fullscreen and print were removed. Both produce the same file: a **3 × 5 in PNG at 300 DPI (900 × 1500 px)** with the DPI written into the file (`png-dpi.js` inserts a `pHYs` chunk, since `canvas.toBlob()` records none). It isn't a screenshot of the on-screen card: a clone is laid out on an off-screen `.camp-card-export` stage 900px wide, repainted from the same content (tags re-trimmed at that size, QR redrawn at print resolution) and captured with `html2canvas` at scale 1. The Classic layout's diagonal lines are square-tiled `linear-gradient`s rather than `repeating-linear-gradient`, and the lanyard hole is a flat disc with a rim rather than an inset shadow — `html2canvas` can't draw either of the originals. A CSS rule also keeps `html2canvas`'s 1×1 font-metrics probe `<img>` inline despite the global `img { display: block }` reset — without it every exported word was ~0.4em too low.
> - The "X / Twitter" field (CC1) asks for just a **handle**, not a full URL — resolved to `https://x.com/handle` when building the QR/visible link.
> - **CampBuddy's wordmark, not its icon, on every layout.** The footer watermark under the QR is now `public/media/logo.svg` (the wide "CampBuddy — WordCamp companion" lockup) on all six cards, sized by height so it's never squished. On the five darker/coloured layouts it sits in a white chip (the wordmark's dark-maroon lettering vanishes on charcoal or ochre); on Pass, whose card is already light, the chip is dropped and it's the same quiet, 80%-opacity logo as before. There is one `.camp-card__brand-mark` element now — the separate `-full` variant and its show/hide switching were removed.
> - **The "Edit Camp Card" form was rebuilt** (`partials/form-field.blade.php`, the shared `_form.scss` / `_pill.scss`, the editor-only `_camp-card-form.scss`, and `camp-card.js`) around four titled groups instead of one flat list — **About you** (name, role/title, company/community, city), **Your interests** (interest tags + "ask me about"), **Find me online** (LinkedIn, website, WordPress.org, X handle), **On your card** (which link the QR points at; which fields show). Every field is built the same way: label above, 44px+ control at 16px type (smaller makes iOS Safari zoom on focus), a hint underneath, and an error line that only appears when needed; controls carry the right `autocomplete` / `inputmode`. A line at the top repeats CC5: it's saved on this device only.
> - **Interest tags** live inside the input box with a proper × remove button on each. Add with Enter, comma (typed or pasted — "a, b, c" becomes three tags), or an **Add** button (a phone keyboard's comma is a long-press away). Duplicates are ignored case-insensitively, up to **8** tags of **30** characters (a counter says how many are used, and a message explains the cap), Backspace on an empty box removes the last, and a row of **tap-to-add suggestions** (Gutenberg, Block themes, WooCommerce, Performance, Accessibility, Design, Security, SEO, Plugins, Community) hides whichever are already chosen and disables at the cap.
> - **Required to generate a card: a name and at least one link** (CC1's "all optional except name", CC3's QR and CC6's empty state taken together — `renderAllPreviews()` keeps showing the sample card until both exist). On **Save**, whatever is missing is **highlighted** rather than silently ignored: the Name field gets a red border/ring and an inline message, and the whole **Find me online** group is outlined in red with a "add at least one link" message; focus jumps to the first problem (name, else the first link field), a "Fill in the highlighted fields…" toast appears, and **nothing is saved** until it's fixed. The marks clear as soon as the person starts typing. Name and Find-me-online carry a ***** and a line above the Save button says what's needed. The form is `novalidate` because native `required` bubbles don't show at all on iOS Safari — the check lives in `camp-card.js` (`validateRequired()`), and the invalid-field style is the shared `[aria-invalid]` ring in `_form.scss` (plus `.form-group--invalid` for the group).
> - **Links are forgiving, then checked.** A URL typed without `https://` gets it added on blur (`linkedin.com/in/me` → `https://linkedin.com/in/me`; a bare domain has no trailing slash), something that can't be a link is flagged beside its field and blocks the save (focus jumps to the first bad one), and the X field accepts `@handle`, `handle` or a pasted x.com / twitter.com profile link and stores the bare handle. The "QR code links to" chips fade for any link that isn't filled in yet. Choice chips show a ✓ when selected (not colour alone) and expose `aria-pressed`. Saving shows a "Camp Card saved." toast as well as collapsing the editor.
> **Current implementation note (design + print quality):** every layout now carries the event's mark and name at the top (so a card found a week later still says where you met), a "Hi, I'm" greeting (not on Pass, which has its tier band), name/role/tags centred in the free space, and a "Scan to connect on LinkedIn / visit my website / …" line under the QR matching where it points. On-screen hints (the "Nothing chosen to show yet" pill) never reach an export or print. **Download and Share** produce a **600 DPI** PNG (1800 × 3000 px for 3 × 5 in, DPI stamped in the file), the QR drawn at that size rather than scaled up.  Only Share and Download are offered (a separate Print / PDF button was tried and removed at the product owner's request). Tags that don't fit collapse into "+N", or disappear entirely rather than being cut off.

> **Ticket layout (30 Sep 2026).** A 7th layout, first in the gallery, modelled on the CampBuddy print ID card (`marketing/id-card`). It has a wine band with the line-icon pattern (`/media/pattern-icons.svg`), rings, and the event favicon in an ochre-rimmed tile, with the event name under it. A dashed tear line with a notch at each edge (paper-soft on screen, white in the PNG). The cream half: "HI, I'M" (ochre, spaced), the name in Inter 800 wine with an ochre rule, role · company, interest pills, and "Ask me about …" in a wine speech bubble. At the bottom, a white dashed panel with the QR beside "Scan to …" and the CampBuddy logo. Only Ticket has `data-ask-bubble`, so there `camp-card.js` takes Ask-me-about out of the pills (`content.tagsBesideAsk`, `paintAsk`); the other six layouts show it as a pill, as before. Extra markup (`.camp-card__rings`, `.camp-card__notch`, `.camp-card__ask`) is hidden on the others. Export verified: 1800×3000 PNG at 600 DPI. The owner confirmed it twice.

> **All layouts reworked (30 Sep 2026, owner confirmed twice).** (1) **No "+N" pill:** every chosen tag shows. `paintTags` steps the type down (`FIT_STEPS` → `--fit-tags` for the pills first, then `--fit-text` for the role and name, to a floor of 0.62 / 0.74) until the body fits. Only past the smallest size does it drop pills from the end, with no "+N". (2) **Compact QR row on every layout:** a grid with the QR (cc(80), about 2 cm in print) on the left and "Scan to …" plus the CampBuddy logo beside it. (3) **Role and company on their own lines** (`.camp-card__role > span`), so no "·" hangs at a line end. (4) Lanyard layouts get more room: the hole sits at cc(10) and the body padding-top is cc(40). (5) **Share / Download:** line icons `share` and `download` (new in `LineIcons`), labels in `[data-export-label]` (`setLabel`), two equal buttons that together are as wide as the card. (6) **Gallery:** one row that scrolls sideways on every screen size, with snap. Each item is a bordered paper panel (`.camp-card-scroll__item`, 264–344px) holding the label, the card and the buttons. Verified with light and full data (8 interests + city + ask), and in PNG exports.

> **900 DPI (30 Sep 2026, owner confirmed).** Share and Download now export at **900 DPI**: `EXPORT_SCALE` is 3, which gives 2700 × 4500 px for 3 × 5 in, with the DPI stamped in the pHYs chunk. The QR is drawn at `260 × EXPORT_SCALE` px. The page note and the toast say 900 DPI. Measured: about 0.75–1 MB per PNG. On a phone it takes about 2× as long to make as 600 DPI did (the "Preparing…" / "Ready, tap to …" flow covers this). This replaces the 600 DPI in the notes above.

> **Shared painting + a person's card (9 Oct 2026, owner confirmed twice).** Drawing a card and making its PNG moved from `camp-card.js` into `camp-card-paint.js` (`cardContent`, `paintCard`, `cardPng`, `saveBlob`, `tapStillCounts`; same 900 DPI, same flow), and a card face's markup into `partials/camp-card-face.blade.php` (the Camp Card page's HTML is unchanged — checked whitespace-insensitive). Explore → People uses both for a **person's card** (`person-card.js`): see [3.3](03-roster-ingestion.md). One fix rode along: Ticket's empty "Ask me about" bubble came out as a blank wine blob in the PNG (html2canvas turns the bubble's `::after` tail into a real element, so `:empty` no longer matched); `paintAsk` now sets `hidden` on it as well.

> **CC5 narrowed by owner decision (9 Oct 2026, confirmed twice): "Show my Camp Card on the attendee list".** A switch on the Camp Card page (off by default; `card-share.js`) is now the one way Camp Card data reaches the server, and only by the attendee's own act. Turning it on needs a saved card (name + a QR link); they pick themselves on the event's attendee list (or, if this phone already linked a name in discovery, that name is used). What is sent is only what is **on** the card — role, company, city, interests, "ask me about", each only if chosen under "Show on my card" — plus the link the QR opens (`sharedFields`), never the rest of the form. Server: `shared_camp_cards` (one per list entry, owner token hashed, `expires_at` = retention end), `POST/PUT/DELETE /api/v1/events/{slug}/camp-card-share[/{shareId}]` (`SharedCampCardController`; a name someone linked in discovery can only be used with that profile's own id + token; first come otherwise). Save updates the shared copy; switching off deletes it; "Clear my data" deletes it before clearing; the card goes with the list entry (cascade) when the person leaves the source Attendees page or is suppressed (hidden), and an admin can take it off (Admin → Roster → *Remove card*). On the list: a *Camp Card* badge, and the person's sheet shows **their own card** (no "made from public info" line, their QR link). The privacy line above the form now says so. GA `camp_card_share` (`result`: on / off) — never the content.
