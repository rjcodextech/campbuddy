# How it works, from your side

[← Back to index](../SKILL.md) · Previous: [What it does](02-what-it-does.md) · Next: [Who it's for →](04-who-its-for.md)

1. **You open the app** — no account, no signup, no password. It just works, right in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen like any other app.
2. **You tell it a little about yourself, if you want** — what you're into, who you'd like to meet. Every question is skippable, and it stays private to you unless you choose to use the matching or sharing features.
3. **You pick your event** — CampBuddy shows you a short list of upcoming WordCamps to choose from. Once you pick one, everything you see (schedule, people, sponsors) is specific to that event.
4. **It guides you** — a home screen that always tells you what's relevant right now, a checklist to work through, a schedule to plan, and people to meet.
5. **You use it during the event** — check off your progress, bookmark sessions, get reminders, show your Camp Card when you meet someone new.
6. **You keep what you want, and nothing you don't** — everything you entered stays on your phone. You can export it or wipe it clean whenever you like.
